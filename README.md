## Official & Planning

Essential official tourism boards, entry requirements, visa guidelines, and meteorological tracking.

- [Visit Hungary (Hungarian Tourism Agency)](https://visithungary.com/) - Official national tourism portal featuring destination guides, cultural events, and thermal bath highlights.
- [Consular Services (Ministry of Foreign Affairs)](https://konzinfo.mfa.gov.hu/en) - Official portal for Schengen visa entry rules, passport regulations, and consular assistance.
- [OMSZ Weather (HungaroMet)](https://www.met.hu/en/) - Official national meteorological service providing weather forecasts, storm warnings, and climate radar.

## Transportation

Navigating national rail lines, intercity buses, river transit, and urban public transport in Budapest.

- [MÁV-START (Hungarian Railways)](https://jegy.mav.hu/) - Official national railway booking platform for intercity trains (InterCity) and international connections.
- [Volánbusz](https://www.volanbusz.hu/en) - National long-distance bus and coach operator linking towns across Hungary.
- [BKK Budapest Transport Centre](https://bkk.hu/en/) - Official public transit authority managing Budapest's metro, trams, trolleybuses, buses, and Danube river boats.
- [Budapest Airport (BUD)](https://www.bud.hu/en) - Primary international aviation gateway with direct 100E express bus and rail links to central Budapest.

## Destinations & Regions

### Budapest & Central Hungary

- [Budapest Info (Official City Portal)](https://www.budapestinfo.hu/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the Parliament, Buda Castle, ruin bars, and museum circuits.
- [Budapest Thermal Baths Portal](https://www.budapestgyogyfurdoi.hu/) - Official visitor hub for historic thermal baths including Széchenyi, Gellért, and Rudas.

### Lake Balaton & Transdanubia

- [Balaton Tourism Board](https://balatonregion.hu/en/) - Official regional guide for Lake Balaton resort towns, sailing routes, and the Tihany Peninsula.
- [Visit Pécs](https://www.pecs.hu/en/) - Visitor portal for exploring southern Hungary's Roman ruins, Early Christian necropolis, and cultural quarter.

### Northern Hungary & Wine Regions

- [Tokaj Wine Region Guide](https://www.tokaj.com/) - Official visitor resource for exploring historic wine cellars, vineyards, and UNESCO-listed cellars producing Aszú wine.
- [Eger Tourism Portal](https://eger.hu/en) - Official travel guide for Eger Castle, Baroque architecture, and the Valley of the Beautiful Woman wine cellars.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers and remote professionals taking advantage of Hungary's White Card residency program.

- [Hungary White Card (National Directorate-General for Aliens Policing)](http://www.bmbah.hu/) - Official immigration portal detailing the 1-year residency permit for digital nomads from outside the EEA.
- [KAPTÁR Coworking Budapest](https://kaptar.hu/) - Popular community coworking space and networking hub located in central Budapest.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contacts, medical assistance, and local safety rules.

- [112 General Emergency Number](https://www.police.hu/) - Integrated European emergency hotline for police, fire, and ambulance dispatch across Hungary.
- [Budapest Tourist Police Assistance](https://www.police.hu/) - Dedicated police division offering multi-language support and traveler safety assistance.
